Society, enriched by all, gains breadth of vision and capacity, and is thus able to seize opportunities and overcome obstacles.

In short: real Democracy exists only if the People enjoy a true Republic. If there exists an institution through which anyone may register and stand as a candidate to contribute to the Public Thing, allowing the regular renewal of those who administer it.
