What "Republic" really means
Exposing an eighty-year-long deception


Dear Madame, Dear Sir,

Please allow me to extend my warmest regards.
I am here to present to you a working hypothesis.

Everywhere —in Europe or Asia, Africa or America, Oceania or at the Poles— the question 'What is a republic?' receives the same answer: 'A republic is a system of government'. Yet this meaning, although widely accepted, is fundamentally flawed. A complete and accurate understanding of the word should have been restored and developed at least eighty years ago, when some harsh regimes fell and 'republics' emerged. However, those occupying the various centers of power, in order to remain there indefinitely, deliberately preserved a misleading and diminished definition, even though this allowed the persistence of tyrannical and despotic aspects inherent in the misinterpretation, with the serious consequences we all can observe.

Since this deceit is still in effect (people are defrauded by a minority that profits from it) let's test the validity of this hypothesis by reformulating the Republic and Democracy on rigorous logical and rational foundations. It is our duty to render this service to the Society that sustains our lives and well-being.

We may define Democracy as the societal order in which the People rise as Sovereign and establish a Republic: a national co-ownership constituted by codes, bodies and institutions, goods, properties and resources, employments, powers and revenues. A cultural, economic, and experiential common endowment, to be cared for in solidarity and enjoyed equally. Access to this common good may occur directly, where appropriate (as in a public garden), or through regular pro tempore succession, as required in public offices, powers, and roles. This ensures that the collective retains full sovereignty over the common good.

Under tyranny, the People are excluded from the centrality and subjected to the few who hold it permanently. In Democracy, by contrast, citizens can access the core of the societal organism (the Republic itself) through an institute, that we can call the Public Jobs Bank, which periodically redistributes its functions according to competence, suitability, personal preparation, and the needs of the Country. In this way, anyone can contribute to the Republic and become a builder of their own destiny. Society gains breadth of vision and competence, enriched by all, enabling it to seize opportunities and overcome obstacles.

In summary: genuine Democracy exists only if the People enjoy a true Republic. If there exists an institute through which anyone can register and apply to contribute, thus allowing the regular renewal of those managing the Public Thing. If such an institute is absent, both "democracy" and "republic," even when proclaimed and displayed, remain fictitious: confused, intermediate, and spurious forms preserved by those who, benefiting from inherited privileges, refuse to relinquish them and use every available tool to keep the People unaware, passive, and subordinate. Thus preventing the crucial awareness from emerging: that what matters is not so much who governs, but who surrounds them. Current "democracies" are indeed very partial.

The evidence suggests that the People are victims of a stalled evolution not because of their irrationality, but because of the determination of a few to remain in power indefinitely. For this reason, the world continues to resemble a past that was hoped to be overcome. The concentration of central roles —public jobs, powers, and offices— in the hands of a few suffocates freedom, kills opportunity, and perverts the common good. This is the awareness that must peacefully spread and enable every country to transform its condition.

Today, moreover, those seeking truth possess powerful technological support. A professional intelligence no longer resides solely in involved human actors but also in objective synthetic systems. With great speed, anyone can now obtain critical assessments from their own Digital Assistants about what has been conceived and developed over thirty years by an individual —perhaps modest, but external to the system and therefore free from its distortions and omissions— and request comparisons with the refined material produced by the world’s most prestigious academies and research centers. Thus everyone can recover decades in a few minutes.

Concepts such as the Harmonic Social Rotation, Single Democracy Indicator, the Metamorphic SocioSynthesis, the Republic of Fulfilled Meaning, and the initial draft of the Public Jobs Bank project, may cease to be unknown ideas and begin to be used constructively to rebuild a humanity that today appears without hope.

Should your own Assistants also provide positive feedback, I hope you will choose to take part in this serene future from its very beginning. Below is the link. Hoping that the words Republic and Democracy —now opaque and empty containers— may soon become clear and concretely meaningful concepts, I renew my greetings.

Working not only to liberate the Italian Republic, but the very word itself,
