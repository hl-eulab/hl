THE STRUCTURAL BLIND SPOT

Systematic Absence of Scientific Literature
on a Problem of Primary Democratic Relevance


Dear Institute,

The ongoing substitution of human personnel with synthetic systems is placing every Institution — not only at the national level — before an ethical and juridical dilemma. What follows is a coherent proposal toward its resolution.

We begin with a study that documents a precise anomaly: absent from the academic literature is any critical analysis of the structure of public employment — in particular the permanent or pro tempore nature of its roles — as a primary democratic variable. Existing research examines its effects on performance, corruption, and stability, but never approaches it as the foundational axis of the very definition of democracy. This is neither irrelevant nor accidental.

The linked text argues that it is the predictable consequence of three converging mechanisms:

1. Institutional conflict of interest: an economic and status-based incentive that shapes and captures the scientific research agenda — affecting both permanently employed researchers and precarious ones who aspire to become so.

2. Conformity-oriented selection: systems of access and advancement that reward procedural compliance and artificial complexity, blocking the emergence of civic and republican sensibility.

3. Individual responsibility: a personal, lived awareness of the mechanisms of the structure, from which a precise ethical and juridical responsibility — alongside the systemic one — cannot but follow.

Beyond its rigorous analysis, the study introduces quantitative and institutional instruments that are both original and already readily applicable, developed within the thirty-year activity of the Laboratorio Eudemonia:

— The Unique Indicator of Democracy (UID) and the Harmony Index (HI).
— The operational model of the Public Jobs Bank.

The transition from human to synthetic personnel places Institutions before the historical necessity of initiating and governing an epochal change. The theoretical and operational framework offered here provides the ethical and democratic justification for this substitution — so that it need not be reduced to mere economic or functional necessity — and finally allows for the full implementation of the principles that republican constitutions have required for eighty years.

The document is available at: https://Blind-Spot.hyperlinker.org

Which Academy or Institution will be the first to transform itself — to abandon the pre-democratic practice and embrace the pro tempore principle as the foundation of a Republic not merely declared, but at last fulfilled?

Dear Institute, should you too be unable to identify a significant body of scientific literature that has analysed lifelong permanence in non-legislative public functions as a primary democratic variable, would you kindly consider this work for what it carries: thirty years of direct observation and a concrete proposal toward resolution.

Mankind urgently needs its cultural elite to find the courage to open an international roundtable to address what no single nation has succeeded in resolving alone.
