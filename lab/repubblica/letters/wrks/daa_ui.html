Introduction

This proposal begins with a fundamental recognition: the management of a country does not reside solely in Parliament, but in the entire Res Publica—that is, in all public employments and offices. It is therefore essential to clarify what has never been properly understood on this subject.

Lifetime employment in the public sector is a practice rooted in pre-democratic times, when power was the immutable prerogative of a closed caste. This practice has survived to this day sustained by bureaucrats and politicians equally determined to cling to power in perpetuity. Public employees hired for life and continuously re-elected rulers have deliberately concealed the unavoidable co-ownership nature of every public job. Such positions cannot be permanently assigned and enjoyed without becoming private property, resulting in a definitive loss for contemporaries. This system has denied many professionally qualified citizens of equal rights the opportunity to participate in the management of common assets.

The result is an immutable, rigid, and oppressive system that has always been suffered, failing to respond properly to life’s necessities or adapt to its continuous and often unpredictable changes. This inadequacy leads to prolonged frustration and, ultimately, violent reactions. Inequality, despotism, and inefficiency generate distrust in institutions and democracy itself, which is effectively murdered by the ancient practice of permanently holding power.


The Vision of the PJB

To leave behind the era of tyranny and establish a true Republic, here I propose the creation of a Public Jobs Bank (PJB)—an institution designed to manage and periodically redistribute every public position. It will ensure continuous renewal of public structures through a digital platform. The PJB thus establishes an open, proactive, and adaptable system, powered by the osmotic principle of a Harmonic Social Rotation.


Goals of the PJB:

- Fair redistribution of public roles, based on candidates selected over time for skills, merits, and needs.

- Collective participation in the management of public assets, by an osmotic process that fosters community and accountability.

- Functionality and adaptability of the public sector, enabling quick responses to crises and economic or social changes.


Legal Foundation of the PJB

The PJB's vision is grounded in a solid legal principle: the co-ownership of public goods (res publica) by all citizens. As common property, no public position can be permanently assigned to any individual or group without depriving others of their right to access it. A system that treats public goods as de facto private property violates this foundational principle of shared ownership, thereby negating the very essence of both Republic and Democracy. The PJB is conceived to eliminate this structural illegitimacy and unequivocally affirm the public, non-appropriable nature of every role within the central and vital nucleus of the society.


The PJB as a DAA: A Constitutional Prototype

To realize this vision, I propose the development of a prototype Public Jobs Bank architected as a Decentralized Autonomous Administration (DAA).

A DAA is a new institutional model: a public administrative body whose core operational logic—for registration, selection, and execution of mandates—is encoded on a transparent, immutable digital protocol (blockchain). Crucially, this technological layer does not replace sovereignty; it executes it. The DAA's legal foundation, democratic legitimacy, and ultimate oversight remain firmly rooted in constitutional law and the principle of popular sovereignty.

This prototype will leverage blockchain and Universal Intelligence not as ends in themselves, but as tools to achieve the PJB's founding principles:

- Democratic Management: Distributed voting mechanisms will ensure decisions reflect collective will and optimize for the common good, maintaining a direct link to citizen-sovereigns.

- Automated Resource Dynamics: Smart contracts will autonomously manage the documented, tamper-proof assignment and rotation of public roles, making the principle of temporary service a reality.

- Aligned Incentives: A non-speculative, internal token will reward constructive participation, skill development, and stewardship of the system, fostering a sustainable ecosystem of engaged citizenship.


UI and Optimization

The integration of Universal Intelligence (UI, or AI, if you prefer a reductive term) within the PJB will leverage advanced algorithms to analyze and optimize the assignment of roles, continuously adapting to societal needs and fulfilling the legitimate desires of participants. UI will ensure fairness, identify inefficiencies, and provide data-driven insights for the continuous improvement of the system.
